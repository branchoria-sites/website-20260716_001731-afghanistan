## Introduction

In September 2013, the island of Thakandhoo in the [Maldives]({{ 'when-supernatural-fear-gripped-the/' | relative_url }}) experienced one of the country's clearest documented examples of a private supernatural crisis developing into a wider community scare. Four children from the same family were believed by many islanders to be possessed by evil spirits. What began as an attempt to treat the children through religious healing quickly expanded into a search for hidden sorcery, police investigations, house searches and growing suspicion directed at members of the community. Although contemporary reports describe intense local belief that black magic had been used, no evidence was produced showing that anyone had caused the children's condition through supernatural means. The episode remains important because it illustrates how fear, rumour and communal suspicion can spread even without verifiable proof, making it a significant case in the social history of collective belief in the Maldives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/politics/police-conduct-witch-hunt-on-thakandhoo-65383" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentPolice conduct witch hunt on Thakandhoo after children allegedly possessed by evil spirits - Maldives IndependentSept...</span></span></span>

## What happened to the four children?

The incident centred on four siblings aged between nine and fourteen who reportedly began displaying sudden and unusual behaviour. According to island residents interviewed at the time, the children experienced episodes that observers interpreted as signs of possession. Reports described fainting, shouting, threatening language, unusual movements and claims by one child of seeing dark figures. Family members and neighbours regarded these events as extraordinary because the children had not previously been known for behavioural problems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/politics/police-conduct-witch-hunt-on-thakandhoo-65383" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentPolice conduct witch hunt on Thakandhoo after children allegedly possessed by evil spirits - Maldives IndependentSept...</span></span></span>

Believing that the children were under spiritual attack, residents invited practitioners from the nearby island of Ihavandhoo to perform Quranic healing rituals commonly known as *ruqyah*. During these sessions, Quranic verses were recited over the children. Local sources reported that the practitioners warned the family that the children's behaviour might initially worsen before improving, reinforcing expectations that dramatic reactions were part of the healing process.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/politics/police-conduct-witch-hunt-on-thakandhoo-65383" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentPolice conduct witch hunt on Thakandhoo after children allegedly possessed by evil spirits - Maldives IndependentSept...</span></span></span>

The practitioners also claimed that black magic objects had been hidden on the island. Following their guidance, residents dug at a location near the island school and reported finding clay tablets or similar objects believed to have been used in sorcery. For believers, the discovery appeared to confirm that the children had been targeted through supernatural means. No independent evidence established that the objects had any connection to the children's illness or that they had been placed there as instruments of black magic.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/politics/police-conduct-witch-hunt-on-thakandhoo-65383" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentPolice conduct witch hunt on Thakandhoo after children allegedly possessed by evil spirits - Maldives IndependentSept...</span></span></span>

## How suspicion became a community search

The Thakandhoo episode became more than a family crisis because attention rapidly shifted from caring for the children to identifying a supposed human culprit.

Community meetings were reportedly held as anxiety spread among residents. Island officials described people as frightened and uncomfortable, and discussion increasingly focused on discovering who might have performed the alleged sorcery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/politics/police-conduct-witch-hunt-on-thakandhoo-65383" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentPolice conduct witch hunt on Thakandhoo after children allegedly possessed by evil spirits - Maldives IndependentSept...</span></span></span>

The police were then called to the island. Acting under court warrants, officers searched several homes for materials that might be connected with black magic, including books or other suspicious items. Contemporary reporting stated that no arrests were made and that searches failed to uncover evidence linking residents to the alleged possession. Some books were reportedly taken away for examination, but no publicly documented criminal case established that anyone had caused the children's condition through supernatural practices.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/politics/police-conduct-witch-hunt-on-thakandhoo-65383" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentPolice conduct witch hunt on Thakandhoo after children allegedly possessed by evil spirits - Maldives IndependentSept...</span></span></span>

One feature that made the incident especially significant was the way existing social and political divisions appeared to become entangled with the supernatural accusations. An island council source told journalists that some residents had deliberately directed police attention towards supporters of the opposition Maldivian Democratic Party (MDP), suggesting that political rivalries may have influenced who became suspected. While these claims came from contemporaneous reporting rather than later judicial findings, they illustrate how accusations of hidden wrongdoing can become attached to existing community tensions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://minivannewsarchive.com/date/2013/09/12" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: minivannewsarchive.com">[minivannewsarchive.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">minivannewsarchive.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">September 12, 2013 – Minivan News – Archive...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 12, 2013</span></span></span>

## Why mass hysteria remains unproven

Thakandhoo is often discussed as an example of a collective possession scare, but describing it as proven "mass hysteria" would go beyond the available evidence.

Several facts are well documented:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Four children displayed behaviour that neighbours interpreted as possession.
* Religious healers performed Quranic recitations.
* Residents searched for evidence of black magic.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/black-magic" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[maldivesindependent.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Maldives Independent Black Magic</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentBlack Magic - Maldives Independent...</span></span></span>
* Police searched homes under court warrants.
* Community suspicion expanded beyond the affected family.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/politics/police-conduct-witch-hunt-on-thakandhoo-65383" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentPolice conduct witch hunt on Thakandhoo after children allegedly possessed by evil spirits - Maldives IndependentSept...</span></span></span>

</div>

Much less certain is the cause of the children's condition.

No medical investigation published findings explaining the children's symptoms, and no court established that black magic had been practised against them. Likewise, there is no evidence that the reported buried objects were scientifically linked to the children's behaviour. Because the available information comes primarily from contemporary news reporting rather than detailed medical or psychological investigation, historians cannot confidently classify the event as either genuine spirit possession, deliberate fraud, mental illness or an outbreak of mass psychogenic illness.

Instead, the strongest evidence supports a narrower conclusion: belief that supernatural harm had occurred became socially contagious. Once the initial interpretation took hold, additional events—including ritual healing, the discovery of alleged magical objects, rumours about suspects and police involvement—reinforced that shared belief and encouraged further collective action.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/politics/police-conduct-witch-hunt-on-thakandhoo-65383" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentPolice conduct witch hunt on Thakandhoo after children allegedly possessed by evil spirits - Maldives IndependentSept...</span></span></span>

## Why the episode remains culturally important

The Thakandhoo case occupies a distinctive place in Maldivian social history because it shows how quickly supernatural explanations can move beyond private belief into public institutions.

Rather than remaining a family matter, the alleged possession led to:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* organised religious intervention;
* community meetings;
* searches for physical evidence of sorcery;
* police investigations under judicial warrants; and
* accusations directed at identifiable members of the community.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/politics/police-conduct-witch-hunt-on-thakandhoo-65383" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentPolice conduct witch hunt on Thakandhoo after children allegedly possessed by evil spirits - Maldives IndependentSept...</span></span></span>

</div>

The episode also foreshadowed later Maldivian incidents in which fears about black magic, cursed objects or sorcery prompted police investigations or public controversy. Seen together, these events suggest that beliefs about harmful supernatural forces can become intertwined with local politics, neighbourly distrust and official responses, even when evidence remains inconclusive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maldivesindependent.com/black-magic" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maldivesindependent.com">[Maldives Independent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maldivesindependent.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Maldives Independent Black Magic</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Maldives IndependentBlack Magic - Maldives Independent...</span></span></span>

## What the Thakandhoo scare demonstrates

The Thakandhoo possession scare is best understood as a documented example of collective suspicion rather than proven supernatural activity or proven mass hysteria. The available evidence shows how unusual behaviour in a small number of children triggered a sequence of reinforcing responses: religious healing, interpretation through existing beliefs about sorcery, searches for physical signs of black magic, widening accusations and official police action.

Its importance lies less in determining what happened to the children than in showing how communities under stress can construct shared explanations for frightening events.
